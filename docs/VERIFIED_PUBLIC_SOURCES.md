# Verified public sources used by Avelune Health

**Research snapshot checked: 10 November 2026.** These sources were reviewed before adding the linked public information to the prototype. Links are provided so users can confirm current details.

## Kenyan cancer-care institutions

- [National Cancer Institute of Kenya — facility portal](https://portal.ncikenya.go.ke/en/facilities): official national portal listing cancer-care facilities and public facility contact information. Used as the source for the five facility records in the starter directory.
- [Kenyatta National Hospital — medical services](https://knh.or.ke/index.php/medical-services/): official hospital page describing its Cancer Treatment Centre and services including chemotherapy, hormone therapy, immunotherapy, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, brachytherapy, pain and palliative care, and patient navigation.
- [Kenyatta University Teaching, Referral & Research Hospital — oncology services](https://www.kutrrh.go.ke/service/oncology-cancer-care-services/): official hospital oncology page describing surgery, chemotherapy and radiotherapy.
- [National Cancer Institute of Kenya](https://www.ncikenya.go.ke/): national cancer institute and current notices. Its October 2026 notice describes a nationwide inspection of cancer management facilities, and a joint notice says facilities seeking SHA cancer-service contracting need valid NCI certification. A listing alone must not be treated as proof of current certification or SHA contracting.
- [KMPDC registers](https://registers.kmpdc.go.ke/): regulator portal for licensed practitioners and health facilities. Check named clinicians and facilities directly before displaying them as verified.

## General cancer-treatment education

- [US National Cancer Institute — Types of Cancer Treatment](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types): authoritative plain-language overview of treatment modalities. It explains that treatment choice depends on cancer type and extent and that people may receive combinations of treatment.
- [Kenya National Cancer Treatment Protocols, 2019](https://uploads.ncikenya.go.ke/uploads/policy/National-cancer-treatment-Protocols-2019.pdf): Kenyan national protocols covering clinical evaluation, diagnosis, imaging, surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, hormone therapy, psychosocial support, palliative care, rehabilitation and survivorship.

## Data-quality policy

1. Only public institutional information is included; no private medical records or personal information scraped from social media.
2. Facility contact details are institutional contacts published through the NCI Kenya portal, not personal clinician numbers.
3. Facility service lists are not guarantees of current availability, operating hours, eligibility, fees, insurance acceptance, NCI certification or SHA contracting.
4. Individual clinicians must be checked against the KMPDC register and the institution before Avelune displays them as verified.
5. The directory is a dated research snapshot, not an automatically refreshed live feed. Re-check official sources before each refresh and store source URL, verification date, service evidence and reviewer.
6. Treatment descriptions are educational only. Belle must not recommend a specific treatment, drug, dose, regimen or treatment sequence. These decisions belong to the treating oncology team.
7. Real medical records must be provided by the patient or an authorised provider through a secure consent-based integration. Never create synthetic records that could be mistaken for real records.
